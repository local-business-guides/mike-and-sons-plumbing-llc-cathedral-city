A practical repair checklist

Plumbing repairs may involve a fixture or another plumbing component, depending on what an assessment shows. A useful visit begins with a clear account of the symptoms and access to the affected area. If you’re preparing to call, consider this short checklist:

  • Describe what is not working as it normally does.
  • Note when you first noticed the change.
  • Say whether the issue is constant or comes and goes.
  • Mention any related changes elsewhere in the home.
  • Make the area reachable if it’s safe, and keep children or pets away from active water.

Avoid forcing a handle, removing a fixture, or using a chemical product as a first response if you’re unsure how it may affect the plumbing. A quick attempted fix can make the original issue harder to identify. If you’ve already tried something, simply tell us what you did.
