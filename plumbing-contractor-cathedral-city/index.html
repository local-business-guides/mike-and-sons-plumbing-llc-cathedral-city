What to ask and prepare

A plumbing contractor may assess fixtures, visible plumbing components, drainage concerns, or other parts of a home’s plumbing, depending on the job. The right work plan depends on the condition found, so avoid deciding in advance that a particular part needs replacement.

Before calling, gather a few details:

  • Note when the issue began and whether it has changed.
  • Identify which fixture or rooms are affected.
  • Share any recent plumbing work or changes you know about.
  • Keep the area around the concern accessible if you can do so safely.
  • Write down questions about the assessment, work steps, and what you should expect afterward.
